FOODON organism-class taxon restrictions

Decision and scope

Retain the 1,679 reviewed FOODON biolink:in_taxon projections with relation=RO:0002162, literal direction, endpoints and FOODON provenance. These are projections of native ontology class restrictions, not assertions that a taxon classification is an individual organism in another taxon. This documentation and regression package does not edit transformed or merged KG rows, add a validator exemption, or claim strict Biolink conformance.

The finite diagnostic cohort has 1,679 distinct subjects and triples: 1,663 targets are NCBITaxon IDs and 16 are FOODON organism classes, including cultivars. Each native OWL subject explicitly carries a subclass existential restriction using RO:0002162 someValuesFrom the target. Thirty-five subjects also express the same restriction in an equivalent-class intersection. All 1,714 matching restrictions are existential; none is universal.

An in_taxon restriction does not authorize same_as, exact_match or a new subclass_of edge. Only 15 of these pairs separately have an asserted native subclass axiom to the same target. Those independent assertions are not a reason to convert the other 1,664 restrictions into hierarchy edges. FOODON targets must not be replaced by guessed NCBI species identities.

Strict schema limitation

The pinned Biolink 4.4.2 schema lists RO:0002162 as an exact mapping of in taxon, with domain thing with taxon and range organism taxon. Its organism taxon category descends from named thing, without the thing with taxon mixin. KGM’s organism-branch category projection therefore produced the historical strict domain diagnostic for these class-level restrictions. This historical cohort includes the separately reviewed wheat-kernel subject FOODON:00005106, now categorized as Food by its own native-evidence correction. Its valid in_taxon restriction is retained. The exact rebuilt graph must be validated again; 1,679 is the reviewed historical cohort, not a promised new validator finding count.

Preserve and report that diagnostic. Do not relabel all organism classes, weaken the model, suppress validator output or infer taxon identity to obtain a superficially clean validation result. This is a finite, evidence-backed disposition, not a blanket exception for every OrganismTaxon in_taxon edge.

Reproducible evidence

tests/resources/foodon_taxon_constraints/manifest.json records the exact original FOODON JSON/OWL, RO JSON, pinned model and review-cohort identities. The finite excerpts contain complete selected OWL subject declarations, native endpoint declarations, ancestry edges and their original JSON coordinates, historical assertion fields and the pinned model fragment.

tests/test_foodon_taxon_constraints.py reparses all complete selected OWL declarations, rejoins literal assertions/provenance, verifies directed ancestry against native edges, distinguishes the 15 separate subclass assertions, and tests wrong direction/property/source/quantifier, annotation-as-axiom, fabricated ancestry and invented hierarchy. A real ontology source-normalizer test preserves all 1,679 rows unchanged. Tests do not consult production data.

The native full-source reconciliation and independent plan review found 1,679 direct restrictions plus 35 equivalent-intersection witnesses, with no unresolved logical blank-node references in this cohort. Fresh producer conservation and model/path/KGX reviews of the exact rebuilt archive remain required; these tests alone do not establish release acceptance.
