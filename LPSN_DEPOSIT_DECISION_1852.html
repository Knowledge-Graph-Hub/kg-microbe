LPSN deposit placement decision — 2026-11-14 PST

The owner approved the narrow correction in #1852, implemented in PR #1849. Existing kgmicrobe.strain:* deposit identities remain biolink:OrganismTaxon. Only their LPSN deposit-to-taxon edges change from biolink:in_taxon / RO:0002162 to biolink:subclass_of / rdfs:subClassOf.

This follows the named-strain taxonomy convention in #834, preserving shared BacDive identities, native type-deposit evidence, direction and provenance. INSDC sequence membership remains in_taxon; publication references remain mentions / IAO:0000142. BacDive’s safe identity links and conflicting-deposit suppression are unchanged. Individual culture-material or aliquot modeling would require a separate identity migration and is outside this correction.

This decision does not establish strict Biolink 4.4.2 domain/range conformance: subclass_of still declares OntologyClass endpoints, while these nodes retain OrganismTaxon. The repository’s documented exception remains visible; no global category change or validator relaxation is introduced. The original #1852 finding remains valid historical evidence.
