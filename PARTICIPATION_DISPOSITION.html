What the evidence supports

The complete selected RO JSON supplies RO:0004007 subPropertyOf RO:0000057 and RO:0002565 subPropertyOf RO:0040036 subPropertyOf RO:0000057. FOODON supplies OBI:0000293 subPropertyOf RO:0000057 directly. The original stronger relation must survive each broader predicate projection, with the original subject, object and supplier evidence. Plain has_participant alone does not convey movement, primary input/output or a plan-specified input.

RO:0000056 and RO:0000057 have a formal inverse axiom. The selected FOODON OBI:0000293/OBI:0000295 declarations contain subproperty axioms and a textual inverse definition, but no formal inverse axiom between those OBI properties. The direct subproperty witness suffices; do not invent an inverse proof.

The full available FOODON/PATO/UBERON OWL files establish existential restriction forms for 314 historical assertions, including two referenced blank-node forms. The remaining nine ENVO assertions are independently verified OBOGraph class projections; the selected raw tree has no ENVO OWL counterpart after an ignored-inclusive search. No independent OWL quantifier proof is claimed for those nine. The two quarantined GO statements also occur under their original subjects in native OWL: their defect was later subject transfer.

A subclass_of (R some B) is not an instance-level relationship between the class identifiers. It does not entail B subclass_of (inverse(R) some A): some B instances may never participate in any A. Generic KG paths must not silently reverse that quantification or infer that every organism performs a process. capable_of and relational-quality towards are not participation evidence just because endpoint categories appear compatible.

Pinned-model limitations remain visible

Biolink 4.4.2 describes participation between a continuant and a process, but sets the continuant-side domain/range to occurrent. Both slots inherit related to at instance level. The checked-in excerpt preserves those actual fields, including the inconsistency; the local model is not patched.

Valid category corrections are not a substitute for those semantics. LifeStage is an OrganismalEntity, not an Occurrent subtype in this model. ENVO includes physical/environmental processes that are not necessarily biological processes. 133 endpoint occurrences (128 distinct supplier/ID pairs) lack explicit paths to the checked BFO roots in their supplier imports. Missing imported axioms do not demonstrate biological incompatibility, but also do not prove universal domain/range compatibility. Strict findings must remain reported explicitly.

Evidence and regression boundary

tests/resources/participation_disposition/ contains a hash-bound property/model excerpt, a separate 323-row historical ledger, and seven representative native OWL/OBOGraph contexts. Original file SHA-256 values and exact native JSON coordinates are preserved. Endpoint excerpts in the ledger deliberately retain only ID, label, class type and native coordinates; they are not complete native ontology exports. Complete property and selected OWL declarations remain in the other fixture files. No live service or mutable raw directory is needed for the hermetic tests.

Tests check native subproperty direction, exact versus broader projections, the literal model discrepancy, historical/quarantine conservation, native blank nodes and a counterexample to invalid class-edge reversal. They also exercise the common ontology normalization stage on all 321 retained assertions. That bounded test is not a substitute for full standard-producer execution, GO admission, or a model/path review of the rebuilt archive.

The #1322 regression controls join every historical subject, object, original relation, projected predicate, supplier and endpoint declaration to its native evidence and pinned-model mapping. The two quarantined GO subject transfers are checked separately through their explicit original-subject records. Selected complete OWL examples, including referenced blank nodes, are reparsed to verify their existential witnesses rather than trusting extracted metadata. Property paths must rejoin the selected native supplier edges, and OWL annotation links must not be treated as logical subclass restrictions. Nine contradictory-evidence tests refresh the fixture manifest and still require semantic rejection; a refreshed hash is not scientific validation.

This package introduces no runtime allowance and makes no post-merge edits. The remaining temporal and FOODON taxon-restriction cohorts have separate dispositions; the approved 292 native-RO retention policy is not expanded here.
