This record is an acceptance ledger, not a statement that a new KG is release-ready. Preserving source occurrence identity and quantity through emission and merge was an additional blocking gate, now addressed by #1171 and independently tested; unchanged identity-resolution keys alone do not establish lossless recipe coverage.
